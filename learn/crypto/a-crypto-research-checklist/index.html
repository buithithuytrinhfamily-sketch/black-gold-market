A crypto research checklist

Crypto academy · Crypto safety

Crypto safety

The idea

Separate the asset's purpose, supply, control, liquidity and custody requirements. Distinguish verifiable facts from promotional forecasts. Consider how the position would be exited and what could prevent access. A checklist organizes questions; it does not certify an asset as suitable or profitable.

A worked example

A compelling product story can coexist with concentrated token ownership and shallow liquidity. Those are separate research questions.

Try it yourself

Create a one-page research note with facts, uncertainties and reasons to decline, without making a purchase.

A mistake to avoid

Treating a community's enthusiasm as independent verification of technical or financial claims.
