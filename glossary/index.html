Trading glossary

80 plain-language definitions for forex, gold, risk and crypto. Search by term or browse by topic.

ABCDEFGLMNOPQRSTVWX

A

ATR

Average true range, a measure of recent range size that incorporates gaps rather than predicting direction.

Link to this term

Ask

The price available to buy an asset from the quoted market, subject to size and execution conditions.

Link to this term

B

Backtest

A simulation applying stated rules to historical data, subject to biases and execution assumptions.

Link to this term

Base currency

The first currency in a pair; EUR is the base in EUR/USD.

Link to this term

Bid

The price available to sell an asset into the quoted market, subject to size and execution conditions.

Link to this term

Blockchain

A linked transaction record maintained under a network's consensus and validation rules.

Link to this term

Breakout

A move beyond a defined price boundary under an explicit observation rule.

Link to this term

Bridge

A mechanism connecting assets or messages across networks, adding its own security assumptions.

Link to this term

C

CPI

A consumer price index tracking changes in a defined basket; methodology and coverage depend on the publisher.

Link to this term

Carry trade

A position seeking a financing or yield difference while accepting exchange-rate and other risks.

Link to this term

Commission

A transaction charge that may apply on opening, closing or both sides of a trade.

Link to this term

Confirmation

Evidence that a transaction has been included and further accepted under a network's rules.

Link to this term

Correlation

A statistic describing co-movement in an aligned sample; it is not causation or a fixed future relationship.

Link to this term

Custody

The arrangement determining who controls keys or holds assets and who bears the related responsibilities.

Link to this term

D

DeFi

Financial applications using blockchain-based contracts, often with multiple technical and economic dependencies.

Link to this term

Depeg

A departure of an asset's traded price from the reference value it is intended to track.

Link to this term

Divergence

A mismatch between extremes in price and an indicator over selected observations.

Link to this term

Drawdown

The decline from a previous equity peak to a later value, often expressed as a percentage of that peak.

Link to this term

E

Equity

Account value including unrealized gains and losses under the platform's accounting rules.

Link to this term

Expectancy

The average outcome implied by win probability, average wins, average losses and costs in a sample or model.

Link to this term

F

FOMC

The Federal Open Market Committee, responsible for US monetary policy decisions within its mandate.

Link to this term

False breakout

A break followed by a return inside the previous boundary under a specified rule.

Link to this term

Fibonacci retracement

Levels drawn at selected fractions of a chosen swing; their usefulness depends on context and testing.

Link to this term

Forward test

A test using new observations after the rules are defined, often initially on a demo account.

Link to this term

Free margin

Equity remaining after required margin, subject to the broker's calculation rules.

Link to this term

Fully diluted valuation

Price multiplied by a stated maximum or total supply measure, which may differ from circulating supply.

Link to this term

Funding rate

A periodic transfer mechanism between derivative positions under the venue's rules; direction and amount can change.

Link to this term

G

Gas

A measure or fee concept for network computation on some blockchains; it is not a universal fixed charge.

Link to this term

Gold futures

Exchange-traded contracts with defined quantity, expiration and settlement terms.

Link to this term

L

Leverage

Exposure relative to account equity, which magnifies gains and losses in equity terms.

Link to this term

Limit order

An instruction to trade at a specified price or better, with no guarantee of a fill.

Link to this term

Liquidation

Forced closure or transfer of a position when collateral requirements are breached.

Link to this term

Liquidity

The ability to execute transactions in size without materially changing available prices.

Link to this term

Long

A position that generally benefits from a rise in the asset or base currency relative to its quote.

Link to this term

Look-ahead bias

Using information in a historical decision that would not have been known at that time.

Link to this term

Lot

A standardized contract quantity whose size depends on the instrument and broker.

Link to this term

M

MACD

An indicator based on differences between exponential moving averages and a signal average.

Link to this term

Margin

Collateral required to support an open position; it is not the same as maximum potential loss.

Link to this term

Margin call

A warning or action triggered when account margin conditions fall below defined requirements.

Link to this term

Market capitalization

Price multiplied by a specified supply measure; it does not equal available exit liquidity.

Link to this term

Market order

An instruction seeking execution at available prices, without guaranteeing the quoted price.

Link to this term

Moving average

A rolling average of observations that smooths a series and lags new price changes.

Link to this term

N

NFP

US nonfarm payroll employment, a measure published in the monthly Employment Situation report.

Link to this term

O

OHLC

Open, high, low and close prices for a defined interval.

Link to this term

Overfitting

Selecting rules that match noise in the development sample and may fail on unseen data.

Link to this term

P

Perpetual futures

Derivative contracts without a fixed expiry, commonly using funding and margin mechanisms.

Link to this term

Pip

A conventional price increment, commonly 0.0001 for many FX pairs and 0.01 for many JPY pairs; check the symbol specification.

Link to this term

Pivot point

A reference level computed from prior-period prices under a specified formula.

Link to this term

Point

A platform's quoted increment, which may be smaller than a conventional pip.

Link to this term

Position sizing

Choosing quantity from a risk budget, invalidation distance and contract specification.

Link to this term

Private key

Secret signing information that can authorize control over assets; sharing it can surrender that control.

Link to this term

Q

Quote currency

The second currency in a pair, used to express the price of one unit of the base currency.

Link to this term

R

R multiple

A trade result divided by its initial planned monetary risk.

Link to this term

RSI

A momentum indicator based on the balance of recent upward and downward price changes.

Link to this term

Range

A period in which price oscillates between approximate boundaries without a sustained trend.

Link to this term

Real yield

A yield adjusted for inflation or inflation expectations under a stated measurement method.

Link to this term

Resistance

An area where selling previously interrupted a rise; it is not a guaranteed ceiling.

Link to this term

Risk of ruin

The chance of reaching a defined unacceptable loss threshold under a specified model and assumptions.

Link to this term

Risk-reward ratio

A comparison of planned loss distance with planned gain distance; always state which quantity is first.

Link to this term

S

Safe haven

An asset sought during some periods of stress; the label does not guarantee protection in every crisis.

Link to this term

Seed phrase

Recovery words used by some wallets to reconstruct keys; never disclose them to unsolicited support or websites.

Link to this term

Short

A position that generally benefits from a fall in the asset or base currency relative to its quote.

Link to this term

Slippage

The difference between an expected execution price and the price actually obtained.

Link to this term

Smart contract

Code executed under a blockchain's rules, potentially exposed to bugs, permissions and dependency risks.

Link to this term

Spot gold

Gold priced for near-term settlement; retail products using spot quotes can have different contractual structures.

Link to this term

Spread

The difference between an executable bid and ask quote.

Link to this term

Stablecoin

A crypto asset intended to track a reference value, with mechanism, liquidity and redemption risks.

Link to this term

Staking

Committing assets under a protocol's rules, potentially involving rewards, restrictions and penalties.

Link to this term

Stop order

An order activated when a trigger condition is reached; execution can differ from the trigger price.

Link to this term

Stop-limit order

An order that activates a limit instruction after a trigger, with a risk of remaining unfilled.

Link to this term

Stop-out

A broker's forced closure of positions under its margin rules.

Link to this term

Support

An area where buying previously interrupted a decline; future reactions are uncertain.

Link to this term

Swap / rollover

A financing adjustment associated with carrying a position across a broker's rollover time.

Link to this term

T

Token

A digital unit defined by a network or contract; its rights, purpose and risks depend on its design.

Link to this term

Token allowance

Permission for a contract to spend a specified quantity of a token on behalf of an address.

Link to this term

Trend

A directional pattern in price over a chosen timeframe.

Link to this term

Troy ounce

A precious-metal mass unit of approximately 31.1035 grams, different from an everyday avoirdupois ounce.

Link to this term

V

Volatility

The extent of price variation over a specified interval; it does not specify direction.

Link to this term

W

Wallet

Software or hardware used to manage credentials and interact with digital assets.

Link to this term

X

XAU/USD

A quotation of gold against the US dollar, commonly expressed per troy ounce; contract terms vary.
