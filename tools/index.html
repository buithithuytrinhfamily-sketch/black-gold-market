Gold position size calculator

YOUR PLAN / ILLUSTRATIVE INPUTS

Size is rounded down to your lot increment. Check contract size, minimum order size and lot increment with your broker. Spread, commissions, slippage and gaps are excluded; actual losses may exceed this estimate. This does not check available margin.

How the calculation works

Risk budget = account balance × risk percentage. Lots = risk budget ÷ (entry-to-stop distance × ounces per lot). Reward-to-risk compares target distance with stop distance. A stop below entry represents a long trade; a stop above entry represents a short trade.
